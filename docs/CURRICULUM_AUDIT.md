# Curriculum Audit — V1

## Coverage and reachability

- Every required universal, Resolve, BMCC, M50 and Ronin subject is explicitly present in the map.
- Graph validation reports no duplicate IDs, missing prerequisites, cycles or unreachable required nodes.
- Every skill includes objectives, three substantive theory blocks, examples, misconceptions, a retryable check, practical assignment, expected deliverable, rubric, evidence fallback, mastery rules, XP and references.
- Final completion requires all domain bosses plus the final project; therefore no optional branch can conceal required completion.

## Source and claim audit

- DaVinci target: stable free **DaVinci Resolve 21**, verified against Blackmagic Design support/training material on 2026-07-30. Studio-only AI and premium capabilities are not required by any assignment.
- BMCC target: original 2.5K MFT model. Verified official facts used in the map: 13 stops, 2.5K/1080 acquisition, CinemaDNG and ProRes, internal SSD, 23.98/24/25/29.97/30 fps, passive MFT/manual lens implications. Exact media compatibility can vary by SSD firmware/model and must be field-tested.
- Canon M50 mode, autofocus and codec details can vary by region and firmware; lessons direct the learner to confirm the camera's active menu/manual and do not generalize Mark II behavior to the original M50.
- Ronin-M payload, firmware and assistant behavior must be confirmed against the installed firmware and official manual; no lesson promises that an untested BMCC rig will balance safely.

## Justified exceptions

General craft lessons cite a professional foundational reference at skill level and teach conditional judgment instead of unsupported absolutes. A future content version may add per-paragraph citations, but no unsupported numerical claims are embedded in generated theory blocks.
