# FilmCraft Academy V1.1 Revision

## Lesson-content repair

The generic three-paragraph lesson factory was removed. All 142 skills now render a minimum of five teaching sections: craft model, topic mechanism, field workflow, diagnosis/correction, and creative use/limits. Topic branches contain concrete material for shutter timing, ISO/noise, dynamic range, white balance, focal length/perspective, depth of field/focus, lighting falloff, gain staging, Resolve project/ingest/Fusion/Fairlight/RAW workflows, Ronin balance, Canon autofocus, and related craft decisions.

Reference packs now total 299 records and prioritize Blackmagic Design, ARRI, Canon, DJI, CIE, EBU, BFI and established professional craft texts. Validation rejects legacy generic prose, lessons under five sections, and skills with fewer than two sources. Creative claims remain conditional; version-sensitive equipment/software facts are tied to official documentation.

## Two-account login

- `SinbodWayne` and `KyanWayne` authenticate locally.
- Only salted SHA-256 verification hashes are stored in source; plaintext passwords are absent.
- Session identity lives in session storage and clears on logout/browser-session end.
- IndexedDB progress keys are account-specific.
- The old `primary` progress record migrates once to SinbodWayne, preserving prior work without exposing it to KyanWayne.
- Browser QA awarded SinbodWayne theory XP, logged out, signed in as KyanWayne, and verified KyanWayne remained at 0 XP.

This is private local access control, not server-grade authentication. Anyone with operating-system/browser-profile or source-code access can potentially alter a local application. Internet deployment should add authentication at the hosting/backend layer.

## Talent Tree redesign

“Worlds” is replaced in navigation and primary routes by “Talent Trees.” The new renderer uses tier bands, original hexagonal FilmCraft nodes, graph connections, locked/available/active/mastered/boss states, zoom, horizontal mobile panning, selected-talent inspector, prerequisite status, mastery rewards, and account identity.
