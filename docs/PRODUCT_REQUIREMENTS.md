# Product Requirements

FilmCraft Academy is a private, single-user, responsive desktop/Android PWA that rebuilds broad filmmaking mastery through an interconnected RPG learning journey. It teaches universal theory, real DaVinci Resolve 21 Free workflows, and hands-on operation of the BMCC 2.5K MFT, Canon M50 and DJI Ronin-M.

## Required outcomes

- Guided freedom through prerequisite-gated, non-linear skill trees.
- Mastery requires theory, a check/reflection, practice, rubric and evidence/reflection.
- Durable local progress, JSON export/import and no required media upload.
- XP, 100-level curriculum-aware progression, titles, specializations, achievements, bosses and cosmetics without spendable points.
- Twelve complete product views and an installable, accessible PWA.
- A portfolio and codex that remain useful after completion.
- Final gate: **BEGGIN' FOR FUEL — Cinematic Music Video**.

## Explicit exclusions

No production scheduling, budgets, call sheets, crew administration, continuity management, arbitrary skill purchases, large mandatory uploads, or refresh-recommended state.

## Acceptance

All canonical skills validate, are reachable, contain substantive lesson/check/practice/rubric/reference content, and support a coherent route to 100%. Automated checks, production build, desktop/mobile rendered smoke tests and documented setup/export/backup/deployment must pass.
